An AI tutoring wrapper that ingests a SKILL.md for tutoring behavior preferences and your class transcripts in order to maximize the efficiency and direction of your studying.  The tutor is able to emulate professor methodology, pick up on what content types are emphasized, and cite content directly from the lectures.  Runs on API key credits.
